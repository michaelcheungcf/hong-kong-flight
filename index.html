Body Motion Flight Simulator

HK Sky Runner

Fly a plane above Hong Kong using your own body movement. A webcam tracks your shoulders, trunk or lower limbs with MediaPipe, and maps your range of motion to the controls of the aircraft.

Victoria Harbour Tsim Sha Tsui Central Tsing Ma Bridge HK International Airport

1Move to fly

Choose a body part, calibrate your full range of motion, then lean or lift it to steer the plane up, down, left and right.

2Collect coins

Golden coins float at different altitudes over the harbour. Fly through them to collect and hear the chime.

3Race the clock

Pick a 5, 10 or 15 minute flight, or go unlimited. Every episode is scored and saved to your log.

Best in Chrome or Edge. A webcam is required. You will be asked to grant camera access.
